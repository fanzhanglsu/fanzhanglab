Join the Lab

Graduate students

Prospective graduate students interested in microbiology, aging biology, host–microbe interactions, imaging, genetics, or data science are encouraged to reach out. Please include a CV, a brief statement of research interests, and unofficial transcripts if relevant.

Postdoctoral researchers

Potential postdoctoral candidates should email a CV, a short cover letter, and contact information for references. Applicants with backgrounds in microbiome biology, computational biology, microscopy, or molecular genetics are especially encouraged.

Undergraduate researchers

LSU undergraduates interested in research training should send a short description of their interests, prior coursework, and availability. Enthusiasm, curiosity, and reliability are highly valued.
