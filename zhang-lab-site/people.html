People

Dr. Fan Zhang

Principal Investigator

Dr. Zhang is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Biological Sciences at Louisiana State University. The lab studies how naturally associated microbes regulate host fitness, endocrine signaling, and aging using C. elegans as a genetically tractable model.

Victoria Rodriguez

Graduate Student

Victoria Rodriguez is a graduate student in the Zhang Lab, focusing on host–microbiome interactions and molecular mechanisms of biological regulation using Caenorhabditis elegans as a model system.
